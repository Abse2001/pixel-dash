The local switched DDR feed and ground return follow the capacitor.

A separate export with that launch installed retained all 50 DDR links, 7 switched CPU supply lands, 18 DRAM supply lands, 183 ground lands and full-width ground coverage outside the launch. Its far end was dangling. The saved board reserves the launch by placement and contains no dangling clock stub. J_CARRIER.55 still needs a continuously referenced connection. An experimental seven-via path conflicts with LCD_RESET, SYSBOOT8, SYSBOOT11 and SYSBOOT13. LCD_D21 remains occupied on its accepted route.

The CPU-side site still conflicts with the right-shoulder trace. Holding that trace fixed eliminated the tested through-barrel path. SYSBOOT9 also lacked a connected corridor in this screen.

For USB, moving the SYSBOOT12 barrel from (27.830, 2.750) to (28.078, 2.535) and jogging JOY_L_CLICK permits paired connector-side launches around (27.220, 2.820) and (27.650, 2.775). Drilled CAM preserves their respective endpoints and the accepted DDR supply, signal and reference results. The experimental USB barrels, trunk and SYSBOOT2/9 changes remain outside the saved board.

### Clock trunk bottlenecks

In a reversible route using both reserved ends, the bottom clock segment crossed SYSBOOT13 between (26.599, 22.135) and (26.717, 22.547). On the CPU side, the front segment crossed SYSBOOT8/SYSBOOT13 around (21.1, 43.7), then SYSBOOT13 again between (23.403, 40.780) and (24.705, 41.209). These are bounds of the screened copper-clearance intersections, in mm. The through sites and the original byte and D21 copper stayed fixed. Allowing only SYSBOOT13 to move yielded a seven-transition clock trunk that displaced 10.116 mm of its trace and one barrel. With that clock installed, the search could not restore SYSBOOT13 from its carrier pull to its CPU launch. These trunks remain excluded.

A second C_DDR27 placement exposed a V5 landing on the opposite side at (24.260057, 39.790087). Both end launches passed isolated drilled clearance and retained C_DDR27 and all DDR endpoints. Even allowing guarded segments on the reference layers with room for adjacent ground, the two endpoint regions remained disconnected in the bounded search. The accepted V5 placement remains the eastern one. The Select and right-stick screens also found no route from contacts 17 and 22 to the original or alternate unassigned GPIOs with the installed bank copper fixed.

### SYSBOOT and LCD D17 trials on 3ae2502

With the mapped two-ended pixel-clock trunk fixed, a local SYSBOOT13 trial tested an L4 shortcut from the carrier pull to its returning perimeter run and a multilayer bypass of its bottom trace at (26.6, 22.3). An independent SYSBOOT8 trial tested taps on both sides of the front crossing near (21.1, 43.7), allowing paired changes of escape layer and barrel position. Neither bounded search restored the respective strap to both ends with the installed clock trunk and reference clearances. No V5 launch was changed.

D17 uses AM3354 V13 and carrier contact 47. A 0.00344 mm length increase in the L3 SYSBOOT1 pull branch clears the connector-side off-pad barrel at (29.568, 4.625). On the CPU side, the bottom oscillator-bias branch increases from 2.97233 to 3.01547 mm to clear the barrel at (31.392297, 39.425965). Its drive and bias resistor connections and continuous L5 ground remain intact. Oscillator startup margin still requires first-article measurement. The saved copper preserves these openings. The coordinates, lead bends and adjacent reference are checked on each export.

In a separate fabrication-film test both D17 launches were installed together. All old pad-island groups were preserved. The dangling ends were the two expected DRC findings. A separate L4 trunk trial joined contact 47 to V13 on drilled copper but interrupted MMC0 CMD near (35, 4) and divided VDD_MPU between its three regulator-side lands and thirteen load-side lands. That trunk is excluded. Both MMC0 endpoints and all sixteen VDD_MPU lands remain together on the accepted layout. D17 remains open.
