# Pixel Dash six-layer CPU module

This separate 37.90 × 46 mm placement uses AM3354BZCZD80 and MT41K512M16VRP-107 IT:P (512M × 16, 1 GiB DDR3L). Open pixel-dash-module.kicad_pro in KiCad 9. The build uses component lands, assignments and outer-layer byte routes from the pinned tscircuit handheld snapshot. On-module functions include TPS65218D0 power conversion, switched CPU DDR power, separate tracking CPU and DRAM VREF dividers, calibration, bypasses, oscillator and reset straps. The carrier supplies VSYS and houses the microSD card, charger, controls and panel. The outline is the result of compacting existing component groups with 0.5 mm inter-group clearance, allowing opposite-face overlap and reserving the underside connector. The smallest routable or mechanically acceptable outline is still open. [Board](pixel-dash-module.kicad_pcb) · [Layout preview](pcb.png) · [BOM](module-bom.csv)

### Fabrication target and connector

Use JLC06161H-1080, nominal 1.6 mm FR-4, outer 0.035 mm foil and inner 0.0152 mm foil. Pressed dielectric thicknesses from L1 to L6 are 0.0764, 0.5500, 0.2104, 0.5500 and 0.0764 mm. L2 and L5 provide continuous ground. L1/L6 carry the byte group and clocks. L3/L4 provide command routing. The candidate uses mechanical L1–L6 holes exclusively. Specify epoxy resin filled, planarized and copper capped at solderable lands (Type VII). JLCPCB lists resin filling as free on six layers and above. Its process limits still apply. The published multilayer preference is 0.20 mm or larger drill. JLCPCB lists a surcharge for any 0.15 mm drill and for 0.20/0.25 mm drills with a land smaller than 0.45 mm. HASL is unavailable for this stack. Finish, small-hole testing, assembly and actual lot pricing require quoting the final data. [JLC via covering](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-via-covering) · [Capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities) · [Published stackups](https://jlcpcb.com/impedance)

The module plug is Hirose DF40C-80DP-0.4V(51), C294544. Its 80 lands use 0.4 mm pitch, 0.20 × 1.00 mm pads and 2.71 mm row spacing. The provisional carrier socket is DF40HC(3.0)-80DS-0.4V(51), C2858577. The distributor index returned $0.8997 and $1.5407 respectively, about $2.44 per pair before assembly. The low-profile C312960 socket returned $0.859, reducing the pair to about $1.76 if carrier relief and verified component heights permit. These are indexed prices without a committed quantity tier. The 3 mm option is a placement assumption. Confirm the mating drawings, worst-case standoff, mechanical support and pin-one transformation on the carrier. Five VSYS contacts and three sequenced 3.3 V contacts are reserved. Check aggregate current, sharing, return drop, inrush and derating against the 0.3 A contact rating before committing the interface. P15 is reserved for USB0 VBUS sensing. The carrier must implement TI's VBUS input requirements including the unpowered case. ### Reproduction

Run verify-export.sh to reproduce the checkpoint checks on the saved PCB with KiCad 9, Shapely 2, Gerbonara, NumPy and SciPy installed. The script reports open items and compares measured results to the saved checkpoint. It also writes a per-net list of physical pad islands.

Run build.py with KiCad 9's pcbnew Python. This writes the project and board, refills planes with the saved project rules, and writes the BOM and pinout. Run extract-geometry.py with the board path to produce the geometry JSON. Use check-timing.py and check-fill.py with that JSON. For independent CAM verification, export the six copper layers and the outline with kicad-cli pcb export gerbers --disable-aperture-macros and the mixed-plating through-drill file with kicad-cli pcb export drill into a scratch directory. Pass that directory and the geometry JSON to check-cam.py. The geometry checks use Shapely 2 and gerbonara. KiCad pcb drc reports opens separately from rule violations.

The bypass placement screen measures each fitted 100 nF capacitor center to the nearest physically connected power and ground balls on its receiver. TI Table 7-65 specifies 10.16 mm at the CPU and 3.81 mm at the DRAM. Via sharing, routed distance to the connection via and effective capacitance require separate checks. The carrier VSYS pin group has a drilled continuity check through the PMIC inputs. Load current, connector temperature rise and rail droop remain open. [Bypass measurements](bypass-placement.csv)


### Six-layer impedance screen

The DDR traces measure 0.102 mm wide on all four signal layers. A two-dimensional electrostatic model uses the published JLC06161H-1080 foil, pressed dielectric, glass styles and solder mask. On a 0.003 mm mesh the isolated outer trace gives 54.987 Ω and the inner trace gives 72.564 Ω even with solid ideal AC references on both sides. TI Table 7-62 specifies ±5 Ω around one nominal impedance. The modeled separation of 17.577 Ω exceeds the entire 10 Ω tolerance band. At the selected 50 Ω nominal, the inner result exceeds the upper limit by 17.564 Ω. The outer result straddles 55 Ω across the two mesh resolutions. This fails the uniform-section screen. Final assessment requires routed cross sections including adjacent etch, plane slots, trapezoidal foil and production tolerances. The existing command capture screen assumes 50 Ω throughout.
