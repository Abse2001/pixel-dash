# Pixel Dash six-layer CPU module

This separate 37 × 46 mm placement uses AM3354BZCZD80 and MT41K512M16VRP-107 IT:P (512M × 16, 1 GiB DDR3L). Open pixel-dash-module.kicad_pro in KiCad 9. The build uses component lands, assignments and outer-layer byte routes from the pinned tscircuit handheld snapshot. On-module functions include TPS65218D0 power conversion, switched CPU DDR power, separate tracking CPU and DRAM VREF dividers, calibration, bypasses, oscillator and reset straps. The carrier supplies VSYS and houses the microSD card, charger, controls and panel. The outline is the result of compacting existing component groups with 0.5 mm inter-group clearance, allowing opposite-face overlap and reserving the underside connector. The smallest routable or mechanically acceptable outline is still open. [Board](pixel-dash-module.kicad_pcb) · [Layout preview](pcb.png) · [BOM](module-bom.csv)

### Fabrication target and connector

Use JLC06161H-1080, nominal 1.6 mm FR-4, outer 0.035 mm foil and inner 0.0152 mm foil. Pressed dielectric thicknesses from L1 to L6 are 0.0764, 0.5500, 0.2104, 0.5500 and 0.0764 mm. L2 and L5 provide continuous ground. L1/L6 carry the byte group and clocks. L3/L4 provide command routing. The candidate uses mechanical L1–L6 holes exclusively. Specify epoxy resin filled, planarized and copper capped at solderable lands (Type VII). JLCPCB lists resin filling as free on six layers and above. Its process limits still apply. The published multilayer preference is 0.20 mm or larger drill. JLCPCB lists a surcharge for any 0.15 mm drill and for 0.20/0.25 mm drills with a land smaller than 0.45 mm. HASL is unavailable for this stack. Finish, small-hole testing, assembly and actual lot pricing require quoting the final data. [JLC via covering](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-via-covering) · [Capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities) · [Published stackups](https://jlcpcb.com/impedance)

The module plug is Hirose DF40C-80DP-0.4V(51), C294544. Its 80 lands use 0.4 mm pitch, 0.20 × 1.00 mm pads and 2.71 mm row spacing. The provisional carrier socket is DF40HC(3.0)-80DS-0.4V(51), C2858577. The distributor index returned $0.8997 and $1.5407 respectively, about $2.44 per pair before assembly. The low-profile C312960 socket returned $0.859, reducing the pair to about $1.76 if carrier relief and verified component heights permit. These are indexed prices without a committed quantity tier. The 3 mm option is a placement assumption. Confirm the mating drawings, worst-case standoff, mechanical support and pin-one transformation on the carrier. Five VSYS contacts and three sequenced 3.3 V contacts are reserved. Check aggregate current, sharing, return drop, inrush and derating against the 0.3 A contact rating before committing the interface. P15 is reserved for USB0 VBUS sensing. The carrier must implement TI's VBUS input requirements including the unpowered case. Independent copper and Excellon reconstruction finds 50 of 50 distinct DDR pairs and zero mapped shorts. The full trace width of every installed DDR signal has continuous adjacent ground outside its own via launch. All 22 byte conductors and the clock pair pass the planar matching and nominal stack-aware skew screens. Worst signed byte skew magnitude is 2.82179 ps against the project 3.663315 ps limit. The TI data mismatch limit is 0.635 mm and differential pair limit is 0.127 mm. CK/CKn mismatch is 0.00011 mm. The installed synchronous command and address lengths are screened against the current placement window of 36.15–38.69 mm. The per-signal file gives the exact length, traversed barrel, flight and skew for this checkpoint. RESETn is asynchronous. Its power-up pulse and release relative to CKE require separate verification. Command-to-clock skew, stubs and package discontinuities still need receiver timing evaluation. The screening model uses 5.884 ps/mm outer isolated, 5.739 ps/mm odd mode, 6.671 ps/mm inner and 6.600 ps/mm traversed barrel. ### Reproduction

Run verify-export.sh to reproduce the checkpoint checks on the saved PCB with KiCad 9, Shapely 2, Gerbonara, NumPy and SciPy installed. The script reports open items and compares measured results to the saved checkpoint. It also writes a per-net list of physical pad islands.

Run build.py with KiCad 9's pcbnew Python. This writes the project and board, refills planes with the saved project rules, and writes the BOM and pinout. Run extract-geometry.py with the board path to produce the geometry JSON. Use check-timing.py and check-fill.py with that JSON. For independent CAM verification, export the six copper layers and the outline with kicad-cli pcb export gerbers --disable-aperture-macros and the mixed-plating through-drill file with kicad-cli pcb export drill into a scratch directory. Pass that directory and the geometry JSON to check-cam.py. The geometry checks use Shapely 2 and gerbonara. KiCad pcb drc reports opens separately from rule violations.

The bypass placement screen measures each fitted 100 nF capacitor center to the nearest physically connected power and ground balls on its receiver. TI Table 7-65 specifies 10.16 mm at the CPU and 3.81 mm at the DRAM. Via sharing, routed distance to the connection via and effective capacitance require separate checks. The carrier VSYS pin group has a drilled continuity check through the PMIC inputs. Load current, connector temperature rise and rail droop remain open. [Bypass measurements](bypass-placement.csv)


### Six-layer impedance screen

The DDR traces measure 0.102 mm wide on all four signal layers. A two-dimensional electrostatic model uses the published JLC06161H-1080 foil, pressed dielectric, glass styles and solder mask. On a 0.003 mm mesh the isolated outer trace gives 54.987 Ω and the inner trace gives 72.564 Ω even with solid ideal AC references on both sides. TI Table 7-62 specifies ±5 Ω around one nominal impedance. The modeled separation of 17.577 Ω exceeds the entire 10 Ω tolerance band. At the selected 50 Ω nominal, the inner result exceeds the upper limit by 17.564 Ω. The outer result straddles 55 Ω across the two mesh resolutions. This fails the uniform-section screen. Final assessment requires routed cross sections including adjacent etch, plane slots, trapezoidal foil and production tolerances. The existing command capture screen assumes 50 Ω throughout. [Per-section results](impedance-screen.csv) · [TI Table 7-62](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/am3354.pdf) · [JLCPCB stack and dielectric data](https://jlcpcb.com/impedance)

TI Table 5-13 gives a typical local MPU network of one 10 µF capacitor and five 10 nF capacitors. The module uses C19702 for the bulk and Samsung CL05B103KB5NNNC, C15195, for the five high-frequency positions. The layout assigns a dedicated 0.20/0.45 mm plated supply and ground via to each capacitor. The DC voltage at the seven balls, effective capacitance and transient droop still require verification. [TI AM3354 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/am3354.pdf) · [Samsung 10 nF part](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C15195.html)

The four joystick axes terminate at the carrier ADS1115 (0x48, ADDR grounded). The module interface assigns the carrier ADC to I²C1 on contacts 62 (SDA, U1.B16) and 63 (SCL, U1.J15), shared with touch. The original single-board design put the ADC on the PMIC I²C0 bus. Carrier implementation must connect the ADC to contacts 62/63, use sequenced 3.3 V for the ADC and bus pull-ups, and check the populated touch controller’s address, bus rise time and reset loading. The two stick clicks use contacts 21 and 22. End-to-end carrier copper and four-axis operation remain open.

For I²C1, configure B16 (SPI0_D1) in mode 2 and J15 (MII1_RX_ER) in mode 3, both with the input receiver enabled. The carrier supplies the open-drain pull-ups from sequenced 3.3 V. B16 uses VDDSHV6 and J15 uses VDDSHV5. Both banks require valid 3.3 V before the bus is used. At reset the B16 pad has a weak pull-up and J15 a weak pull-down. Check bus-high voltage and rise time with those pulls and the installed carrier loads. [TI AM3354 pin attributes and Table 4-49](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/am3354.pdf)
