# Pixel Dash six-layer CPU module

This separate 37 × 46 mm placement uses AM3354BZCZD80 and MT41K512M16VRP-107 IT:P (512M × 16, 1 GiB DDR3L). Open pixel-dash-module.kicad_pro in KiCad 9. The build uses component lands, assignments and outer-layer byte routes from the pinned tscircuit handheld snapshot. On-module functions include TPS65218D0 power conversion, switched CPU DDR power, separate tracking CPU and DRAM VREF dividers, calibration, bypasses, oscillator and reset straps. The carrier supplies VSYS and houses the microSD card, charger, controls and panel. The outline is the result of compacting existing component groups with 0.5 mm inter-group clearance, allowing opposite-face overlap and reserving the underside connector. The smallest routable or mechanically acceptable outline is still open. [Board](pixel-dash-module.kicad_pcb) · [Layout preview](pcb.png) · [BOM](module-bom.csv)

### Fabrication target and connector

Use JLC06161H-1080, nominal 1.6 mm FR-4, outer 0.035 mm foil and inner 0.0152 mm foil. Pressed dielectric thicknesses from L1 to L6 are 0.0764, 0.5500, 0.2104, 0.5500 and 0.0764 mm. L2 and L5 provide continuous ground. L1/L6 carry the byte group and clocks. L3/L4 provide command routing. The candidate uses mechanical L1–L6 holes exclusively. Specify epoxy resin filled, planarized and copper capped at solderable lands (Type VII). JLCPCB lists resin filling as free on six layers and above. Its process limits still apply. The published multilayer preference is 0.20 mm or larger drill. JLCPCB lists a surcharge for any 0.15 mm drill and for 0.20/0.25 mm drills with a land smaller than 0.45 mm. HASL is unavailable for this stack. Finish, small-hole testing, assembly and actual lot pricing require quoting the final data. [JLC via covering](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-via-covering) · [Capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities) · [Published stackups](https://jlcpcb.com/impedance)

The module plug is Hirose DF40C-80DP-0.4V(51), C294544. Its 80 lands use 0.4 mm pitch, 0.20 × 1.00 mm pads and 2.71 mm row spacing. The provisional carrier socket is DF40HC(3.0)-80DS-0.4V(51), C2858577. The distributor index returned $0.8997 and $1.5407 respectively, about $2.44 per pair before assembly. The low-profile C312960 socket returned $0.859, reducing the pair to about $1.76 if carrier relief and verified component heights permit. These are indexed prices without a committed quantity tier. The 3 mm option is a placement assumption. Confirm the mating drawings, worst-case standoff, mechanical support and pin-one transformation on the carrier. Five VSYS contacts and three sequenced 3.3 V contacts are reserved. Check aggregate current, sharing, return drop, inrush and derating against the 0.3 A contact rating before committing the interface. P15 is reserved for USB0 VBUS sensing. The carrier must implement TI's VBUS input requirements including the unpowered case. ### Electrical checkpoint and fabrication hold

Independent copper and Excellon reconstruction finds 50 of 50 distinct DDR pairs and zero mapped shorts. The full trace width of every installed DDR signal has continuous adjacent ground outside its own via launch. All 22 byte conductors and the clock pair pass the planar matching and nominal stack-aware skew screens. Worst signed byte skew magnitude is 2.82179 ps against the project 3.663315 ps limit. The TI data mismatch limit is 0.635 mm and differential pair limit is 0.127 mm. CK/CKn mismatch is 0.02038 mm. The installed synchronous command and address lengths are screened against the current placement window of 36.15–38.69 mm. The per-signal file gives the exact length, traversed barrel, flight and skew for this checkpoint. RESETn is asynchronous. Its power-up pulse and release relative to CKE require separate verification. Command-to-clock skew, stubs and package discontinuities still need receiver timing evaluation. The screening model uses 5.884 ps/mm outer isolated, 5.739 ps/mm odd mode, 6.671 ps/mm inner and 6.600 ps/mm traversed barrel. These coefficients do not establish impedance, receiver PVT or actual launch. [Measured timing](timing.csv) · [Drilled copper and return coverage](drilled-cam.txt)

KiCad found zero copper-rule violations and 131 unconnected items at this checkpoint. 44 additional surface links join local pin and rail islands. Eleven more ground paths connect isolated pads or clusters, including U1.N7, the VTP and ZQ resistor returns, the CPU VREF bypass return and three carrier ground contacts. A subsequent 33 surface routes joined 34 previously disconnected islands, including RTC power-hold and warm reset. Rail current, sequencing and transient response remain open. The separate via-land edge to unrelated track-edge audit found zero clearances below 0.1524 mm on the extracted board. The outstanding connections include signal and power networks. [Spacing audit](check-via-spacing.py) · [Findings](via-spacing-findings.csv) The module has 379 through vias, 240 at 0.20/0.45 and 139 at 0.15/0.30 mm (drill/land). A same-center trial making every hole 0.20/0.45 produced six shorts, 166 clearance findings, 126 hole-clearance findings and one hole-to-hole finding with all track errors enabled. The assembly screen identifies two bores crossing a solder-land edge and eighteen off-center bores contained in a component land. Nominal containment is only a geometric screen. TI SPRS717L §7.7.2.3.3.5 calls for VDDS_DDR plane coverage across the DDR region, in addition to the signal-return geometry. This checkpoint has ground planes on L2/L5 and uses both inner routing layers for commands. L3 now carries the post-inductor DCDC3 feed across a 751.52 mm² island. Twenty off-pad 0.20/0.45 mm feed vias were added. Seven further breakout feeds connect PMIC IN_LS1, bypasses and four DRAM balls. A 3.55 mm L4 rail connection joins the isolated A1/B2/C1/D2 cluster to DCDC3 using existing vias. A8 and C9 use filled-and-capped 0.15/0.30 mm through vias offset by 0.010 and 0.005 mm respectively. The nominal copper land is contained inside each 0.394 mm DRAM pad. Assembly and registration tolerances still need verification. The E9 supply escape changes D6 from 19.14010 to 19.22539 mm with the same two F-to-B barrel spans. D6 has 0.16870 mm mismatch against 0.635 mm and +1.86191 ps worst modeled skew against ±3.663315 ps. Both byte groups and the clock retain passing screens. The F1 escape includes a 2.71 mm L4 link that rejoins a separated rail island. D9 uses a centered filled and capped through via after DQ9 and DQ15 were rerouted with unchanged via spans. Their worst modeled skews are +1.521 and +1.747 ps against ±3.663315 ps. Drilled CAM reaches 18 of the 18 DRAM power balls from the inductor. L4 has a separate switched CPU DDR region from the load-switch output, with dedicated through feeds to bypasses and the CPU reference divider. The drilled report counts physical CPU supply balls separately from DRAM. L3 command slots, antipads and isolated loads still require closure for continuous VDDS_DDR coverage. The return-coverage check measures ground under traces only. [TI AM335x datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/am3354.pdf)

### Reproduction

Run verify-export.sh to reproduce the checkpoint checks on the saved PCB with KiCad 9, Shapely 2 and Gerbonara installed. The script reports open items and compares measured results to the saved checkpoint. It also writes a per-net list of physical pad islands.

Run build.py with KiCad 9's pcbnew Python. This writes the project and board, refills planes with the saved project rules, and writes the BOM and pinout. Run extract-geometry.py with the board path to produce the geometry JSON. Use check-timing.py and check-fill.py with that JSON. For independent CAM verification, export the six copper layers and the outline with kicad-cli pcb export gerbers --disable-aperture-macros and the mixed-plating through-drill file with kicad-cli pcb export drill into a scratch directory. Pass that directory and the geometry JSON to check-cam.py. The geometry checks use Shapely 2 and gerbonara. KiCad pcb drc reports opens separately from rule violations.
