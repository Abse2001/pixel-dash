# A7 and A10 exchange on the populated handheld

This rejected two-net study reinstates A10 on the panel D12 checkpoint after withholding A7. The checked-in circuit is the export input. The A10 layer indices in the route are zero-based, top to bottom. The production build does not incorporate this exchange.

Drilled CAM connects F4–L7 but loses E2–R2, leaving 36/50 DDR links overall. A10 is 27.68967 mm, 8.46032 mm short of the 36.14999–38.68999 mm project window. Estimated flight is 201.517 ps with −34.954 ps skew to CK. The contacted depth of its two L1/L3 transitions totals 2.650 mm. Both bytes and the clock reproduce the e4c906f fingerprints. Source and receiver signoff remain open. Exported L3 falls short under bottom-side D13 by 0.0000278 mm² and D6 by 0.0000297 mm². Copper, drill, via-edge spacing and trace self-contact pass. Moving A7 back to its original route would cross the new A10 wire on L3. The CPU A10 through-via launch also intersects L2 copper beneath the original A7. The search for two 0.102 mm tracks with a 0.102 mm gap finds two bottlenecks around (−48.265, 15.759) and (−48.886, 12.146) mm. In a geometric sensitivity test, reducing ground via 138 from 0.4572/0.254 mm to 0.30/0.15 mm opened the upper passage. The lower passage still failed with A8 temporarily removed. D6 needs L3 reference in that region and D5 needs L2. Neither the via change nor the A8 removal is installed. The archived board retains the original ground via. An alternate layer transition before the lower passage is the next routing problem to solve.
