# A10 filled and capped launch study on f9cb35c

This trial installs the two 30036c3 launches on the accepted populated handheld. The independent Gerber and Excellon audit finds both lands joined to their four-layer barrels. CPU F4 and memory L7 are still separate physical networks. The byte/clock lock, full-width routed DDR and MMC0 references, 0.102 mm unrelated copper, 0.1524 mm via-edge clearance and drill spacing pass. The board has 37 distinct DDR pairs and 1,189 plated barrels. [CAM](exact.log) · [Launches](barrel.txt) · [Reference coverage](ref.log)

Both are mechanically drilled L1–L4 through vias. CPU via offset from its ball center is 0.029090 mm. The radial containment column describes nominal copper artwork only. Drill registration, remaining annular ring after drill wander, solder-mask registration and the finished pad profile need manufacturer validation.

**Fabrication and assembly callout for these solderable BGA lands.** Provide nonconductive epoxy resin fill, planarization and copper cap on both faces (POFV, IPC-4761 Type VII), with the specified solderable BGA pad openings and finish. Verify cap continuity, planarity, fill integrity, finished plated hole size and annular registration for these coordinates. The copper and drill exports cannot verify a physical fill or cap. The board also uses L1–L2 and L3–L4 copper-filled laser microvias and a mechanically drilled L2–L3 buried span.

JLCPCB publishes a 0.150 mm minimum through hole, land diameter at least 0.100 mm larger than the hole, and maximum 16:1 mechanical via aspect ratio for HDI. The nominal through hole gives about 9.94:1 using 1.491 mm finished thickness. Both 0.150/0.300 launches fit those published dimensions and the 0.15–0.55 mm POFV diameter range. Four-layer POFV is a paid option. JLCPCB lists standard resin POFV as included on 6–20 layers. No price, JLCPCB pressed buildup, lot dielectric data, cap thickness guarantee, or acceptance for this combination of pad registration and drill spans has been established. The engineering stack is Würth HDI4_1-2b-1_149_35_2V13, with 0.030 mm copper on each layer, 0.065 / 1.200 / 0.065 mm pressed dielectric and 1.491 mm finished thickness. For launch to L2, L3 or L4, signal barrel travel from L1 is 0.095, 1.325 or 1.420 mm respectively. At the screening coefficient 6.600 ps/mm the one-way contributions are 0.627, 8.745 or 9.372 ps per launch. Two bottom-layer launches contribute 18.744 ps of flight before lateral routing. Exits on inner layers leave unused through-barrel stubs. Stub loading, pad/via discontinuities and the return transfer need signal-integrity analysis. L2 ground and L3 DDR supply are the designated local reference conductors. [Stack drawing](https://www.we-online.com/files/pdf1/en_hdi4_1-2b-1_149_35_2v13.pdf)

With all routed conductors installed, independent searches on 0.040, 0.032 and 0.026 mm grids found no common accessible routing component for the fixed lands. A separate 0.040 mm search extended the field to contain the entire ellipse allowed by the 38.68999 mm project ceiling. With A7 alone lifted the CPU reaches the large routing region. With A6 alone lifted the RAM reaches it. Each opposite endpoint remains isolated. These finite-grid results identify routes to investigate and do not prove continuous-space impossibility. A6 and A7 must both retain their prior command windows for adoption.

## Isolated A10 in-window path

Releasing A7 at the CPU and A6 at the RAM together permitted a through path. A first tuning pocket isolated the L3 supply feed at (−57.8, +5.6) mm. The saved alternative preserves landed copper while adding length. A10 now measures 36.57770 mm against the 36.14999–38.68999 mm window, giving 0.42771 mm lower and 2.11229 mm upper headroom. It traverses L1→L3→L1→L3→L1. All four holes are plated L1–L4. The four signal traversals total 5.300 mm of active barrel (34.980 ps), with 0.095 mm of unused barrel beyond L3 at each transition. The route contains 31.2494 mm of inner-layer lateral travel. Estimated board flight is 274.193 ps and estimated skew to mean CK is +37.722 ps. Receiver setup and hold remain open. The in-window study connects 36 distinct pairs, including A10 but with A6 and A7 open. Both locked bytes and clocks, DDR rails and MMC0 still pass on its drilled CAM. [Saved circuit and checks](a10-in-window)

Overlaying retained A6 and A7 on this path would short three object pairs and violate clearance on a fourth. The top-layer A6 copper intersects an internal A10 via and the A10 trace, while A7 intersects A10 on L3. Fresh A6 and A7 searches on the installed A10 trial returned paths of 50.70578 and 46.52555 mm respectively, exceeding the upper placement limit by 12.01579 and 7.83556 mm even before checking both together.
