## Four-layer through-via conversion study

The trial replaces 36 blind vias with conventional 0.20 mm drilled, 0.45 mm land through vias. Six ground vias clear solderable lands. Thirty are centered in existing component lands, with at least 0.10 mm nominal copper containment. The solderable locations require nonconductive resin fill, planarization and copper cap (IPC-4761 Type VII). These include the J_SD shell, controls and power components. Their signal copper and endpoints retain the accepted geometry. The production build continues to reproduce the accepted fallback. [Thirty capped locations](capped-vias.csv) · [Six off-pad locations](converted.csv)

Drilled CAM still finds 37 independent DDR links, seven MMC0 connections, the DDR supplies and ground, and 38 split handheld nets. All 22 data routes and their 44 vias match the baseline. DDR/MMC0 full-width checks pass and the selected reference-plane paths, raster findings, clock and command timing measures retain the baseline values. The unchanged engineering stack was used to compare geometry. Four-layer JLCPCB stack qualification and receiver validation remain open. Remaining non-through vias comprise 271 L1–L2, 44 L3–L4 and 34 L2–L3.

To reproduce this trial, decompress the saved circuit into an isolated export directory, export its Gerbers with the project exporter, and compare the drilled file spans to the included CAM report. The two via lists contain every changed coordinate. [Board](board.png) · [Drill and connectivity report](drilled-cam.txt) · [Power and MMC0](power-and-mmc0.txt) · [Via coordinates](converted.csv)

All 22 byte routes also pass a provisional standard-stack comparison using the existing JLC04161H-1080 outer-layer field estimates (isolated 5.884 ps/mm, odd mode 5.739 ps/mm). The two 1.530 mm barrel traversals on each route use the 6.600 ps/mm sensitivity coefficient. Maximum modeled absolute skew is 2.82179 ps. The comparison retains the original ±3.663315 ps data and ±0.713359 ps strobe-pair limits. It screens a stack change with the same XY layout. The fabricated stack, impedance corners, full command bus, receiver launch and device timing remain open. Reproduce with `studies/data-bytes/check-lanes.py --jlc1080 baseline.circuit.json trial.circuit.json`. [Per-signal limits and residuals](jlc1080-byte-timing.csv) · [JLCPCB stack options](https://jlcpcb.com/impedance)

A board-wide fixed-center inventory checks both conventional drill classes at each of the 385 original blind or buried barrels. At 0.20/0.45 mm, 103 centers clear both fixed foreign copper and peer drills in an individual change. Of these, 81 also clear foreign pours, and six are clear of solder lands as well. At 0.15/0.30 mm, 162 centers clear fixed copper and drills. These are independent local checks. Changing two adjacent barrels together can change their spacing, and extending a rail into a plane can isolate a downstream feed. The inventory records the affected objects by layer geometry.
