## Four-layer through-via conversion study

The trial replaces 36 blind vias with conventional 0.20 mm drilled, 0.45 mm land through vias. Six ground vias clear solderable lands. Thirty are centered in existing component lands, with at least 0.10 mm nominal copper containment. The solderable locations require nonconductive resin fill, planarization and copper cap (IPC-4761 Type VII). These include the J_SD shell, controls and power components. Their signal copper and endpoints retain the accepted geometry. The production build continues to reproduce the accepted fallback. Drilled CAM still finds 37 independent DDR links, seven MMC0 connections, the DDR supplies and ground, and 38 split handheld nets. All 22 data routes and their 44 vias match the baseline. DDR/MMC0 full-width checks pass and the selected reference-plane paths, raster findings, clock and command timing measures retain the baseline values. The unchanged engineering stack was used to compare geometry. Four-layer JLCPCB stack qualification and receiver validation remain open.
