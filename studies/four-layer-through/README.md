At 400 MHz, command IOCTRL 0x318 selects SR11 and drive code zero for both CK and CA (TI Selector_4 Model_357, nominal 1.60 × Rext). This is a trial register setting. The study has no Pixel Dash boot image that programs it. On the JLC04161H-1080 foil-center delay model, the 13 connected synchronous command nets give 416 load and edge cases. Across all 416 nominal cases the intersection of the modeled source capture windows is 697.806–1993.199 ps. The programmed 1250 ps reference phase lies inside that interval. Absolute pin launch and receiver timing remain open. The two clocks, asynchronous RESETn and all physically open commands are excluded from this count.

Using the TI model's min and max V/T columns and their matching fixture biases, the 1.215 V corner yields a conditional 849.281–1927.304 ps common interval. The 1.485 V corner has zero eligible cases because CK slew is 4.808–5.820 V/ns, outside the 1–4 V/ns table. Both diagnostic corner voltages lie outside the Micron 1.35 V operating range of 1.283–1.45 V. These extremes do not determine the result at the actual rail limits. The calculation assumes tracking VREF, isolated transitions, lumped loads and TI 50-ohm source fixtures. It still needs the operating rail, launch bounds, package/channel behavior and the complete command routing before a PVT signoff.

### Receiver and VREF operating checks

The exact drilled midpoint connects the same divider to AM3354 J4 and RAM H1, with a separate CA branch to M8. The divider supply comes from the DRAM rail. The processor measures its reference against its own switched VDDS_DDR. TI requires 0.49–0.51 of that local rail. At 1.323 V on the DRAM rail, the fitted 1 kΩ ±1% pair allows a 0.505 midpoint. J4 then permits only 12.97059 mV difference between the rails even without leakage, ground offset or noise. At the ideal midpoint the allowance is 25.94118 mV.

The exported switched L4 feed and the CPU E5 row contribute 14.2204 mΩ combined at 20 °C on the nominal copper model. Placing the 250 mA screening load at E5 and using a 40 mΩ switch sensitivity gives 13.5551 mV drop and **−0.2981 mV** J4 upper-limit margin even before adding the omitted feed and return terms. A 25 mΩ switch gives +1.6144 mV in the same partial model. Neither resistance/load combination establishes the assembled rail drop. The permitted supply ranges, return offsets, transient currents, divider loading, tolerance over temperature and shutdown behavior all matter. Separate local tracking for J4 warrants a routing trial. Tightening the shared-divider tolerance alone would still couple the CPU reference to the unswitched rail.
