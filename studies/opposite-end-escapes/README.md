# Opposite-end DDR escape study

These trials start from the populated 740abbe board. Each JSON file contains trial centerlines in mm, ordered CPU to RAM. Layer indices 0–3 correspond to L1–L4. No trial qualifies as a replacement for the accepted copper.

The transition lower bound joins the CPU ball, each installed via center in traversal order and the RAM ball with straight lines. Copper obstacles and tuning can only add planar length for that fixed topology. A4 with BA0 routed has a 45.40919 mm lower bound against the 38.68999 mm ceiling. A2 with A14 restored has a 42.79280 mm lower bound. The A4 relief also cuts two existing DRAM supply via lands off the main L3 pour during the checked fill. These western paths require different launches or transitions. [Per-route bounds and lengths](transition-bounds.csv)

A12 reaches the southern RAM corridor when A7 is movable. The paired A12/A7 routes have lower bounds inside the ceiling, but their L3 clearances conflict between X −49.34 and −48.00 mm and Y 10.04 and 16.08 mm. The coordinates bound the conflicts across multiple runs of each route. The 37.87846 mm A2 exchange only fits when A14 is lifted. Its copper runs within 0.212 mm of A14 copper for 27.011 mm of centerline on L1.

The 0.043 mm search grid spans X −73.002 to −36 mm and Y 3.003 to 30 mm. It subtracts installed metal, existing drill clearances, adjacent full-width return and protected return paths. Reachable area counts cells per layer joined by permitted drill spans. It measures available space in the chosen discretization. A12 reaches 0.227 mm² on L1 at the CPU and zero on deeper layers. Its RAM ball reaches the large component on all four layers. CSn0 has a similar CPU bottleneck. None of the thirteen open nets joined both ends on this screen. A separate trial on eleven nets allowed up to 0.235 mm of locally unreferenced launch within a candidate via's own relief, with physical wire obstacles kept active. This also produced no complete connection. [Both-end area and ball-distance screen](end-capacity.csv)

With A8 lifted, offset L1 entries into the SYSBOOT7 and SYSBOOT10 CPU lands exposed 0.004296 and 0.006932 mm² of start-center area. The calculation requires at least 0.018 mm track-to-pad copper overlap. In the connected L1 pockets, the copper/drill screen leaves 0.136327 and 0.040852 mm² for L1–L2 blind-via centers and zero for full-depth centers. Each result is local. The completed route would also require adjacent reference, DDR supply continuity, the A8 restoration and exact CAM. The accepted board retains A8 and leaves these CPU straps open.

A transverse screen sampled the southern A7 study route every 0.05 mm. Within the contested region, 11 of 112 connected centerline intervals are narrower than 0.204 mm, the minimum center spacing for two 0.102 mm traces with 0.102 mm edge clearance. The narrowest sampled interval is 0.14121 mm near (−48.85331, 12.22331) mm. The L4 D6 reference footprint and the remaining L2 ground coverage bound it. This result applies to that side and direction of approach. [Transverse samples and limiting copper](south-sections.csv)

At SYSBOOT7 and SYSBOOT10, protecting the L2 reference beneath A7 further reduces the blind landing areas to 0.032873 and 0.034164 mm². The closest L3 ground is respectively 3.923 and 3.717 mm from the original blind landing areas. A local pad opening alone does not provide a ground-referenced route out of the package.

A WEn trial kept all six transitions and reduced modeled flight from 287.897 to 287.533 ps, but increased L3 reference area missing outside nominal antipads by 0.883 mm². The accepted board retains the earlier WEn route. A separate RAM-side search on a 0.032 mm grid found 45 candidate landing cells along the final inner section and none with an in-window route to the RAM on L1.
