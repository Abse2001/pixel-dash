# North RAM breakout with reserved boot paths

The RAM and local reference fanout move 1.0 mm north. All sixteen SYSBOOT lands join the intended bias and remain isolated from one another. Drilled CAM decodes 0x4017. The RTC core, RTC I/O, reset, wake, hold, 21 DCDC4 CPU/USB loads, 16 LDO1 loads, CPU returns and MMC0 card-power gate pass. Seven of 29 raster signals reach the panel, including LCD_D0 on the existing SYSBOOT0 sense microvia.

Native Gerbers and drills connect 20 of 50 DDR links. Restoring the old D4 split the only reachable paired bottom-layer corridor between the two DQS0 escapes. Moving D4 allowed a common trunk for DQS0/DQSn0; both legs now measure 16.884 mm with two transitions each. D4, D5 and D7 reach the RAM with two transitions each. The byte-0 timing screen still finds up to 26.434 ps D7-to-strobe difference. The new DQS1 pair measures 18.119 mm per leg with two full-depth transitions apiece. There are no detected Gerber shorts, 0.102 mm copper violations, 0.1524 mm via-edge violations, drill-spacing or courtyard findings. All routed DDR, SD and raster signal widths have connected reference coverage. The length and timing reports use the 1.491 mm reference build and 1.420 mm traversal. D15 differs from DQS1 by up to 28.593 ps in the uniform odd-mode screen. The other 30 DDR links are open.

The study includes the sequenced TPS22975 buffer. The widened L4 feed supplies all seven CPU DDR balls, 22 existing bypasses and the local bulk capacitor. Direct DCDC3 supplies all 18 RAM power balls. At 105 °C, 80% conductor area, 279.59 mA and 40 mΩ switch resistance, the screening reserve is +8.894 mV DC and −2.006 mV dynamic. The alternate supply geometry needs lower impedance. The production branch retains its 50-link layout and independently screened supply path.

See connectivity.txt, lengths.csv, timing.txt and the current-ls1 reports in checks. The whole-board CAM audit reports 64 mapped nets split across copper islands. Bench cold boot and transient verification are pending.
