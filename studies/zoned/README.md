# North RAM breakout with reserved boot paths

The RAM and local reference fanout move 1.0 mm north. All sixteen SYSBOOT lands join the intended bias and remain isolated from one another. Drilled CAM decodes 0x4017. The RTC core, RTC I/O, reset, wake, hold, 21 DCDC4 CPU/USB loads, 16 LDO1 loads, CPU returns and MMC0 card-power gate pass. Seven of 29 raster signals reach the panel, including LCD_D0 on the existing SYSBOOT0 sense microvia.

Native Gerbers and drills connect 25 of 50 DDR links. D12, D14, A10, A12 and CSn0 now reach their RAM lands after the strobe breakout. Restoring the old D4 split the only reachable paired bottom-layer corridor between the two DQS0 escapes. Moving D4 allowed a common trunk for DQS0/DQSn0; both legs now measure 16.884 mm with two transitions each. D4, D5 and D7 reach the RAM with two transitions each. The byte-0 timing screen still finds up to 26.434 ps D7-to-strobe difference. The new DQS1 pair measures 18.119 mm per leg with two full-depth transitions apiece. There are no detected Gerber shorts, 0.102 mm copper violations, 0.1524 mm via-edge violations, drill-spacing or courtyard findings. All routed DDR, SD and raster signal widths have connected reference coverage. The length and timing reports use the 1.491 mm reference build and 1.420 mm traversal. D14 uses six traversals and differs from DQS1 by up to 115.482 ps in the uniform odd-mode screen. Routing D14 before D12 in a separate trial transferred the six-via detour to D12. The other 25 DDR links are open.

The study includes the sequenced TPS22975 buffer. Local masked L1 copper spreads supply current across the BGA lands. The L4 feed clears the board cutouts by at least 0.30 mm and supplies all seven CPU DDR balls, 22 existing bypasses and the local bulk capacitor. Direct DCDC3 supplies all 18 RAM power balls. At 105 °C, 80% conductor area, 279.59 mA and 40 mΩ switch resistance, the screening reserve is +10.973 mV DC and +0.073 mV dynamic. The positive dynamic result is only 0.073 mV in this reference-stack sensitivity. It does not establish a production operating margin. The production branch retains its 50-link layout and independently screened supply path.

See connectivity.txt, lengths.csv, timing.txt and the current-ls1 reports in checks. The whole-board CAM audit reports 59 mapped nets split across copper islands. Bench cold boot and transient verification are pending.
