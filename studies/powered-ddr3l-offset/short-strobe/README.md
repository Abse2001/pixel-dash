# West byte 0, actual pin mapping and strobe window

The assembled west exchange maps U1.N4 to DRAM F2 for D5, P4 to G2 for D7, and M3 to E3 for D0. The maximum Manhattan distance across the mapped byte including mask and strobes is 20.600104 mm at D6. The stored mapping includes currently open lands. The geometric comparison to both original strobes alone requires 20.883457 mm. Changing only data via depth cannot resolve that interval.

Both revised strobe routes keep their original two full-depth vias. With DQS0 at 20.566078 mm and DQSn0 at 20.540032 mm, mismatch is 0.026046 mm against 0.127 mm. The all-case data flight window is 133.726391–137.780675 ps. Across legal planar lengths the possible total data barrel travel is 2.255211–3.454286 mm. Two full transitions give 2.840 mm and permit 19.931078–20.600104 mm of planar routing. Two L1–L2 transitions give 0.190 mm and cannot pass. The 2.650 mm L1–L3 composite is only a delay calculation. It would require an independent landing, return, drill and impedance proof. The physically tested option uses L1–L4.

These are nominal finished copper centerlines from the 1.491 mm Würth HDI4_1-2b-1_149_35_2V13 reference, with L1–L2 at 0.095 mm and L1–L4 at 1.420 mm. The flight sensitivity uses 5.769 ps/mm isolated, 5.617 ps/mm odd-mode and 6.600 ps/mm barrel. Neither the as-fabricated delay nor a JLC impedance coupon has been measured.

The receiver CSV uses the MT41K512M16VRP-107 TwinDie x8 base-die DQ tables. It lists required CPU-ball write setup and hold after board skew, not achieved receiver slack. At DDR3L-800 the AC135/DC90 reference-slew bases are 140/160 ps. At the DDR3L-1866 bin AC130/DC90 bases are 70/75 ps. The conservative 800 MT/s read screening case uses tDQSQ max 200 ps and tQH min 0.38 tCK (950 ps at 2.5 ns). DM is write-only. Actual programmed rate, PHY sampling phase and aperture, launch timing, package loading, slew derating and jitter are required for receiver signoff. [Micron Tables 58 and 60](https://media.digikey.com/pdf/Data%20Sheets/Micron%20Technology%20Inc%20PDFs/MT41K_DDR3L_4Gb_Rev.Q_Dec2017_DS.pdf)

The strobe-only checkpoint preserves the 39 drilled links and passes mapped-net isolation. The subsequent D0 and data group must be verified separately.
