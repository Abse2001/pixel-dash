# DDR3L placement and byte-0 escape on the powered handheld

The source is the 408d38c populated board with the CPU center at (−44.8, 15.15) mm and original RAM center at (−65.0, 15.15) mm. The exported board in this folder is an electrically rejected routing study. The production build still uses the original placement.

The three trials retain the RAM rotation and CPU footprint. The trial generator translated channel coordinates rigidly west of x = −59 mm, tapering the displacement linearly to zero at x = −52.5 mm to give the retained fanout a starting geometry. The surrounding handheld stays fixed. D1–D7 and A1 were lifted. Strobes, D0 and DQM0 remain installed. All trials used the same four-layer reference-stack model, two L1–L4 transitions totaling 2.840 mm, 0.635 mm data matching, 0.127 mm pair matching and the separate ±3.6633 ps worst-case data-skew screen. Placement maxima were recomputed and enforced.

- **RAM center (−64.2, 16.75) mm.** With D0 at 19.3248 mm and DQM0 at 19.4259 mm, the joint planar and flight window is 19.1259–19.8621 mm. The six full-through vias assigned to D2, D3 and D4 have 1.207477 mm minimum center spacing against 0.4020 mm. The isolated study assigns D2 to the former D3 RAM land, D3 to the former D1 land, and D4 to the former D7 land, completing the permutation within the byte. D4's RAM-side via overlaps its assigned RAM land. All six 0.15 mm holes match the L1–L4 plated Excellon file. All 24 layer/land inspections cover a 0.149 mm radius. No D2, D3 or D4 end-to-end length or flight time is credited for these barrels. A 0.045 mm routing grid produced no D5 or D7 via-pair candidates within this window. The separate whole-board CAM audit also detects a short between VDDS_DDR, GND and the select-button net in the shifted study. - **RAM center (−66.2, 15.55) mm.** The joint window is 20.8835–21.5729 mm with D0 at 21.1612 mm and DQM0 at 21.3183 mm. The sampled RAM-side D5 and D7 centers occupy the same narrow region near (−59.45, 10.67) mm. Across 2,016 and 1,728 candidate via pairs respectively, the largest RAM-side distance with distinct RAM destinations is 0.229456 mm, a 0.172544 mm deficit. CPU-side options can exceed the spacing requirement. Moving just the nearby DQM0 segment in sampled local detours produced no candidate meeting its timing and copper clearance together. D5/D7 timing remains unverified since no complete pair was installed. [Pair distances](west-pair-bounds.txt) · [All-net timing](west-timing.csv)

The grid search masks obstacles, reference copper, drill and via clearances, checks connected top/bottom regions and samples up to 36 positions per terminal/component before constructing pairs. The straight-line sum through a pair of vias is only a lower bound on routed length. Failure of this bounded search does not exclude another fanout. The CAM barrel spacing measurement is independent of this sampling limit.
