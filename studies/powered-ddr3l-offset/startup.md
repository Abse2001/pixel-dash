# Powered startup trials from 408d38c

The current SYSBOOT1 resistor is a 10 kΩ part strapped to the sequenced DCDC4 3.3 V supply. Its signal end shares LCD_DATA1 after reset. A fit trial moved its top-side center from (−32.75, 6.00) to (−32.60, 5.90) mm, adjusting both rail segments and the LCD_D1 start in the assembled board. It retained 0.5600 mm minimum package bounding-box separation to the next placed top-side component (R_SYSBOOT2). The populated trial passed 0.102 mm copper clearance, 0.1524 mm via-edge spacing, drill spacing and full-width raster reference.

Exact drilled CAM shows the bias pad on high and R_SYSBOOT1 pin 2 on J_LCD pin 31. U1.R2 stays on a separate island. The physical CPU pull and post-reset CPU-to-panel data connection therefore **both fail**. This is a routing trial and gives no first-article boot or panel timing measurement. A more westerly placement interfered with LCD_D15. The fit-tested move was excluded from the primary board because moving the resistor does not clear the CPU launch. [Powered fit-trial CAM](strap-fit-drilled-cam.txt)

For B4, the archived RTC route from U1.B4 to R_PMIC_WAKE pin 2 (on D7/DCDC6) was tested against the fully populated powered copper. Removing the complete backlight PWM trace and its vias reduces its unrelated layer/object clearance conflicts from five to two. The two remaining top-layer objects are DDR_A6 and DDR_A8. Its summed trace area inside the DDR envelope remains 0.320083 mm² with and without PWM. A separate B4-to-D7 search with PWM absent still finds no legal offset launch at B4. The verified backlight copper remains in the primary design. B4's 1.8 V continuity, RTC reset, wake, and cold boot remain open on that design.

Two independent panel tap trials on the powered layout used a launch inside the rectangular sense pad at R_SYSBOOT6 or R_SYSBOOT12. Each export failed drill spacing by 0.267533 mm (signed edge gap −0.137533 mm against +0.130 mm required) and exposed 0.0043082 mm² of the new top-layer trace outside its inner1 return.
