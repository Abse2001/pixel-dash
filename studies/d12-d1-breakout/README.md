# D12/D1 breakout trial on the populated 50-link board

The fallback is a4a71e2. Run `python studies/d12-d1-breakout/measure.py board.circuit.json` on the rendered fallback to reproduce the geometric lower bounds in [envelope.csv](envelope.csv). The calculation retains all component lands. It suppresses only the selected nets’ traces and vias, then applies the same 0.408 mm keepout margin as the whole-board audit. Negative clearance means the center lies inside the keepout. The exposed-area column measures the actual copper pad outside that margin.

Removing D12 and D1 alone exposes **zero copper** on either N5 or P5. N5 is still 0.515469 mm inside and P5 is 0.367958 mm inside. Their land radius is 0.179997 mm. In the assembled 50-link board, A14 and A10 define the hull edge at N5. A10 defines the edge at P5 after D1 is removed. The earlier D12/D1 edge attribution used the smaller byte-and-clock hull.

In the additional optimistic calculation with A10 and A14 also removed, N5 exposes 0.019415 mm² and P5 exposes 0.077980 mm². These areas precede replacement routing of all four signals and account only for the common envelope. The screened 0.102 mm top feed has just 0.000291 mm² of eligible trace-end centers at N5 after applying unrelated copper, via spacing and terminal overlap. That pocket is isolated from P5 by the switched CPU DDR supply trace. The P5 and P6 pocket does connect. Trial feeds require new power, return and manufacturing checks before adoption.

## Populated two-route trial

The isolated candidate changes two routes and their four through-via positions. The other 48 DDR routes and their vias compare identically to a4a71e2. D1 measures 23.7523 mm (previously 23.7653), and D12 measures 23.6406 mm (previously 23.6978). Each retains 2.840 mm total barrel. D1's worst modeled signed strobe skew is +22.8219 ps and D12's is +7.3702 ps against the project ±3.6633 ps screen. TI's 0.635 mm matching gate also fails on both. The [50-net timing report](trial-timing.csv) gives prior and current flight, signed skew, the applicable length and delay targets and residuals. Command receiver timing remains open.

Independent Gerber/Excellon checks resolve 50 separate DDR pairs, 1,088 plated barrels, all 8,460 source strokes and zero mapped-land shorts. Full-width DDR, MMC0 and routed raster reference checks pass. All 71 local DDR reference terminals reach their planes through 70 blind vias. The common envelope has under 0.000000005 mm² residual per plane outside nominal antipads. The 0.102 mm copper clearance, 0.1524 mm via-edge clearance, drill spacing and DDR self-contact audits have zero findings. The longest cumulative class-spacing excursion is 21.593 mm versus 31.750 mm allowed. Sequenced MMC0 power and all seven signals pass. The [whole-board connectivity audit](physical-lands.txt) still fails with 35 split logical nets, including N5/P5. The [LS1 model](ls1-screen.txt) passes its engineering screen. Physical validation remains open.

A separate four-route-free power search found a possible 0.26 mm L1 connection from L5 to a relocated 0.38/0.18 mm barrel near (−48.437, 14.238) and 1.183 mm of 0.40 mm L4 copper toward the switched DDR rail. This is a proposed feed for examining the N5 obstruction. Exported continuity for all seven switched lands, signal restoration, reference relief and current capacity remain unverified for that variant. The populated two-route trial retains the existing power feed and is archived without promotion.
