This 37-link routing checkpoint retains the prior four-command solution for A5, A6 and WEn. A3 moves one launch and avoids the L2 copper needed by the pre-existing byte return paths. The board includes both complete byte groups and their original 44 vias. All 20 selected return-path results on the exact export reproduce the 36-link measurements, including the two stubs that end within their launch region. These geometric checks establish conductive paths. Transfer impedance and receiver timing remain open.

A3 is 38.14850 mm with 5.490 mm total contacted transition depth, 265.580 ps estimated flight and +29.110 ps relative to mean CK. Its remaining headroom to the project ceiling is 0.54149 mm. A5 and A6 also pass the 36.14999–38.68999 mm window. A9, BA1, CKE and WEn still miss it. All transitions and residuals are in timing.log.

The independent drilled reconstruction identifies 37 distinct CPU-to-RAM connections, 1,123 plated barrels and zero mapped-land shorts. All 5,520 segments match exported copper. The scoped DDR supply, reset-independent memory calibration, VREF and MMC0 checks are recorded in exact.log and whole.log. The direct outer-layer four-width screen retains 24 inherited findings. The conservative envelope retains 57 findings, with L2 and L3 areas outside via relief of 7.645 and 92.746 mm². These include clearances around inner signal copper. The fitted-capacitor distance screen still flags four RAM capacitors, and the legacy local-return source checker expects a single blind-via topology it cannot identify on this board or the fallback. The drilled land checks supply independent endpoint evidence. Whole-board power, eight missing physical boot straps, 39 split nets, coupling, loop impedance, receiver margin and manufacturing signoff remain open. The isolated C41 bypass was not installed.
