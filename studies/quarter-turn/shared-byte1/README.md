# Shared upper-byte escape

D10, D11, D12 and D14 all reach separate RAM balls on the same export. The routed four pass the TI 0.635 mm strobe and present-peer length rules and the separate ±3.66332 ps board-delay screen. Their worst signed model skews are −2.73460, −3.57128, +2.49294 and −3.52362 ps. D11 has just 0.09204 ps modeled headroom. Stack and propagation uncertainty can exceed that headroom. The four use two outer-to-outer transitions per signal, with 2.840 mm total barrel. The lower-byte D2/D3/D4 and both strobes retain their verified copper.

The export verifies eleven physically distinct DDR signals and 1,007 plated barrels, zero mapped shorts, full-width references, all 71 local DDR returns, SYSBOOT0, calibration and the separate DDR supplies. The 39 other DDR signals and whole-handheld electrical closure remain outstanding here. This is a routing study.
