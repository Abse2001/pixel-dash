### Powered 50-link socket-return trial

The study builds from 4cdb79c and reinstalls the saved N5/P5-to-P6 DCDC4 branches from a37b3fe. Two joystick grounds and four holes serving the microSD SHIELD3/SHIELD4 lands now use 0.20 mm through holes with 0.45 mm lands on short L1 leads. Front solder-mask clearance to the six through lands ranges from 0.115001 to 0.135001 mm. Both inner foils cover the entire nominal annulus at each location. The board retains 271 L1–L2 blind, 23 L3–L4 blind and nine L2–L3 buried holes. Existing small-drill and fill requirements still apply.

The original board was regenerated from source. Its circuit JSON reproduced the comparison input byte for byte. Both Gerber sets were exported independently. Drilled CAM verifies 50 distinct DDR links, no short between mapped land nets, all socket ground returns, all 21 sequenced 3.3 V I/O lands including N5/P5, seven switched CPU DDR lands and 18 direct DRAM supply lands. A dedicated gate walks the 39 fitted DDR bypasses and their returns, the VREF divider, both memory references and the processor reference. Full-width DDR and MMC0 references, 0.102 mm copper, 0.1524 mm via-edge clearance and drill spacing pass. Whole-board split nets drop from 35 to 34. MMC0 power and signals pass; only SYSBOOT bits 11, 13, 14 and 15 physically reach the CPU strap lands. Six of 29 display links reach the panel.

The 22-row byte and 28-row clock/command JLC04161H-1080 comparison files reproduce the parent results byte for byte. Both strobe pairs pass their checks. Both data groups fail the combined peer/flight gate, and seven data signals exceed the placement-derived maximum. CK/CKn mismatch is 0.095 mm against the 0.127 mm limit. A6 is 3.200 mm below the command placement window; A10 and CKE exceed it by 8.04082 and 2.58315 mm. Signed flight is estimated; controller launch, hot supply behavior and valid receiver capture across real operating corners remain open. The accepted complete-byte layout remains a separate fallback.

**RTC breakout study.** The existing top route corridor between B4 and D7 splits into separate islands in the 0.025 mm grid screen. A centered 0.15/0.30 mm through via at B4 overlaps L4 CKE and lacks clearance to A2 and A13 even if CKE is removed. At B5 and C5 centered through barrels intersect A6 and A14 respectively on L4. Among top-reachable off-pad B4 centers clearing fixed pads and drills, even the best 0.15 mm bore requires cooperative changes to CKE and another command. The surveyed site at (−49.825042, 21.549988) needs at least 0.14394 mm additional clearance to CKE and 0.16142 mm to A2. These are local geometry screens before plane cuts, routing to the supply or fab tolerance. B4, B5 and C5 remain open.
