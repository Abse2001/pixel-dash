## MMC0 boot and DDR initialization

Pixel Dash specifies a 24 MHz oscillator and a fitted SYSBOOT word of 0x4017. The intended first boot device is MMC0. On ca10e34 eight CPU strap taps are still open. The ROM device selection cannot establish the DDR clock or pin drive configuration. The repository contains no Pixel Dash SPL board target, executable MLO, or executed DDR register trace. An actual pad launch time therefore remains unknown. [Drilled strap connectivity](drilled-cam.txt) · [TI AM335x TRM, chapters 7, 9 and 26](https://www.ti.com/lit/ug/spruh73q/spruh73q.pdf)

TI U-Boot at 70800b68682ddc7a9e7e6695712a94fc9f62ed37 selects a 400 MHz configuration for PocketBeagle or BeagleBone Black board identity. The unrecognized-board branch selects the 266 MHz DDR2 configuration. A Pixel Dash port must select its own DDR3L profile before external memory access. The reference calls the DDR PLL setup, calibrates VTP, programs three command macros and two data macros, writes the I/O controls, hands CKE to the PHY, and configures the EMIF PHY, timings and SDRAM. [Reference board selection](https://github.com/TexasInstruments/ti-u-boot/blob/70800b68682ddc7a9e7e6695712a94fc9f62ed37/board/ti/am335x/board.c) · [Initialization order](https://github.com/TexasInstruments/ti-u-boot/blob/70800b68682ddc7a9e7e6695712a94fc9f62ed37/arch/arm/mach-omap2/am33xx/emif4.c)

The TI reference writes 0x18B to all five I/O registers, 0x80 to each command slave ratio, and zero to each clock inversion register. At 400 MHz, 128/256 of a period is 1250 ps of programmed DLL shift. Actual pin-to-pin launch phase also depends on the PHY, package and operating corner. Capture those write-only ratio and inversion writes in SRAM-resident SPL, along with the clock configuration. The TI timing and data-ratio constants are for MT41K256M16HA. Pixel Dash uses a single-rank, 1 GiB MT41K512M16VRP TwinDie with 16 row, 10 column and three bank address bits. Derive EMIF addressing, refresh and timing from the fitted part and validate the full address range and both byte lanes. ### Reset strap voltage budget

TI lists LCD_DATA0–15 as high-impedance during reset and latches them on the rising PWRONRSTn edge. For these LVCMOS inputs with their I/O rail in the 3.3 V range, VIH(min) is 2.0 V and VIL(max) is 0.8 V. Use 3.135 V at the pull-up for the low-rail corner. The fitted resistors are 10 kΩ. Using 10.1 kΩ (+1%) and allocating 18 µA to CPU high-impedance leakage, a high strap can tolerate another 94.38 µA sinking from the node before reaching VIH. A low strap can tolerate another 61.21 µA sourcing into it before reaching VIL. Both limits have zero voltage reserve at equality. These DC budgets apply only to a connected strap while the rail is valid. An attached unpowered panel can load or clamp the node during ramp. Its transient loading and the reset sampling levels still need verification. [TI AM335x pin attributes, note 5, and §§5.4/5.7](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/am3354.pdf) · [Fitted resistor](../../imports/A_0402WGF1002TCE.tsx) · [CPU-to-strap continuity](drilled-cam.txt)

The original eight open CPU taps each have a fixed copper conflict at a centered 0.15/0.30 mm through via. On the touch bus, a D15 via at the ball center also conflicts with the underside UART RX probe. In a separate study, shifting the probe 0.15 mm in both axes and reducing its diameter to 0.70 mm opens access to L2 and L3. A 0.065 mm grid search using through vias and adjacent ground found no connected route from those exits to the pull-up. Removing mechanical card-detect copper allows a touch-clock path in the earlier mixed-via study but loses the seventh MMC0 link. Those trials have no effect on the accepted boot, debug or touch circuits.
